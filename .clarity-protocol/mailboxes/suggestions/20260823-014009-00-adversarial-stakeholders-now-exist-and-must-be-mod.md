# Adversarial stakeholders now exist and must be modelled

**Source:** conversation
**Target:** goal/stakeholders.md

The document closes with 'No adversarial stakeholder is worth modelling' and a conditional: 'If storage becomes a hosted service, that changes and the analysis should be redone.' That condition has been met, and by a route it did not anticipate — the adversary is lawful compulsion rather than breach, and it applies to a seized device as well as to a hub. Add at least: a compelling authority (can obtain a hosted repository, can seize or image a device, can compel unlock, may act under a gag order), and possibly an opportunistic thief. Also strengthen 'People written about': under a political threat model their exposure may be materially worse than the user's, and they are the group that cannot consent, cannot assess the risk, and cannot be warned.
