# D36's deferral premise no longer holds

**Source:** conversation
**Target:** decisions/decisions.md

D36 defers failure analysis on the reasoning that v1 has no remote, which 'bounds the exposure to one machine'. Against an adversary who can seize or image a device, one machine is not a bound — it is the whole corpus in plaintext. The deferral of the full cycle may still be right, but this specific justification should be marked superseded rather than left standing, and the reopening trigger should move from 'before v2a begins' to 'now', which the current work does in practice.
