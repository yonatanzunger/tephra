# Self-censorship: the writer stops writing what matters

**Source:** conversation

Knowing the history is permanent and potentially compellable, the user stops writing the things that most need thinking through. The notebook remains technically perfect and stops being the place where the hard thinking happens, which is the entire premise of the project.

## Additional Context

The user named this themselves as the Soviet solution. It is the failure mode most likely to actually occur, it is invisible to every instrument, and it defeats success criteria 1 and 2 directly. A security design that produces this outcome has not succeeded — it has relocated the loss from confidentiality to the thinking itself.
