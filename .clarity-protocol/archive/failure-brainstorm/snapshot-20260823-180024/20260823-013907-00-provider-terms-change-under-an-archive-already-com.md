# Provider terms change under an archive already committed

**Source:** conversation

A corpus is pushed under one set of policies, jurisdiction and ownership. Over a twenty-year horizon the provider is acquired, changes jurisdiction, changes retention, or begins scanning content. The archive was committed under assumptions that no longer hold and cannot be un-committed.

## Additional Context

The twenty-year horizon is what makes this different from ordinary provider risk. Any decision about the remote is a bet about a corporate entity over decades.
