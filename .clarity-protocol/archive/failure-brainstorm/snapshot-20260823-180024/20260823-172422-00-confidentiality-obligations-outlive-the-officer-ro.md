# Confidentiality obligations outlive the officer role

**Source:** conversation

The material of concern is 'facts I am not normally allowed to discuss' — NDA'd, privileged, or trade-secret information. Those obligations run with the information, not with any adversary, and typically survive departure indefinitely. Writing such facts into a durable archive creates exposure whose trigger is any future disclosure event at all, not a targeted one.

## Additional Context

This is a third exposure that neither retired adversary covers, and the aggrieved counterparty is the former employer rather than a court or a political actor. Trigger events are mundane: an unrelated suit in which the writer is a witness, a stolen laptop, a provider breach, a future employer's discovery, an estate. The officer role ending removed the subpoena firehose; it did not release the obligations.
