# Losing the key loses twenty years

**Source:** conversation

If the corpus is encrypted at rest, the key becomes a single point of failure over a twenty-year horizon. Losing it destroys the archive completely — the exact outcome the entire project exists to prevent, and worse than any predecessor system, none of which could be lost all at once.

## Additional Context

Directly contradicts success criterion 4 (nothing is ever lost) and the departer stakeholder. Any encryption proposal has to price key custody over decades, including the user's own death or incapacity.
