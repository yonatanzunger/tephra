# A mitigation that does not hold invites freer writing

**Source:** conversation

The user believes content is protected — encrypted, purged, excluded from history — and on that belief writes material they would otherwise have withheld. The belief is wrong in some corner (metadata, a backup, an unprotected derived file) and the exposure is larger than if no protection had been claimed.

## Additional Context

This is the project's own recurring lesson in a new domain: a passing check is evidence of nothing until the check has been seen to fail. Every security property claimed here needs an instrument that has been deliberately broken and seen to report it.
