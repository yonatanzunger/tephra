# Third parties bear a risk they never accepted

**Source:** conversation

People written about have no idea the notebook exists, never consented to its storage, and cannot assess or refuse the risk. Under a political threat model the consequences of disclosure may be materially worse for them than for the user, who at least chose to write it down.

## Additional Context

stakeholders.md already lists this group and calls the storage choice a disclosure decision made on their behalf. The new threat model raises it from a privacy nicety to the ethically load-bearing constraint in the whole design.
