# Encrypted content still leaks a pattern of life

**Source:** conversation

Even with unreadable blobs, a hosted remote knows the account identity, commit timestamps, sizes and cadence. That establishes when the user was writing, how much, and how it correlates with external events — often the fact actually of interest, and enough to establish association.
