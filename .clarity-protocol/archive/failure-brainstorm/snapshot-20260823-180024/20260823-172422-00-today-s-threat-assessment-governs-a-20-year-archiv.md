# Today's threat assessment governs a 20-year archive

**Source:** conversation

The judgement that risk is low and decreasing is a judgement about the present adversary and the present attack chain. Content written under that assessment persists for decades into circumstances that may not resemble it, and cannot be recalled once the assessment changes.

## Additional Context

Applied carelessly this argument proves too much — every archive faces it. What makes it actionable is that it argues only for content scoping, never for architecture: encryption and self-hosting do nothing about a future in which the writer is compelled or the material simply matters more. It therefore supports the cheap answer while narrowing which version of it works.
