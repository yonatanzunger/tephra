# Public politics does not cover private texture

**Source:** conversation

The 'already blown' argument holds for the fact of the writer's politics, which is public and settled. It does not hold for the private working-through: half-formed positions, arguments later abandoned, uncharitable characterisations, the reasoning behind conclusions since revised. That material has rhetorical value to an adversary even when it has no evidentiary value.

## Additional Context

The attack-chain analysis is correct that nobody needs evidence to establish what the writer believes. But the classic use of a leaked notebook is not to prove a belief — it is to embarrass, to discredit, or to characterise intent, and for that purpose private texture is strictly more useful than a public position. Considered writing is defensible; thinking out loud is not written to be.
