# One undifferentiated stream makes narrow disclosure impossible

**Source:** conversation

Legal process demanding material on one subject can only be answered with the whole corpus, because subjects are tags over a single stream rather than separate containers. Twenty years of unrelated writing about uninvolved third parties is disclosed to satisfy a request about one topic.

## Failure Chain

1. A demand arrives naming one subject or one date range
2. Tags are inline markup over one stream; no artifact on disk corresponds to the demand
3. Producing only responsive text means trusting a filter over twenty years of prose
4. The defensible answer and the cheap answer are both 'hand over the corpus' **harm begins**
5. Everyone ever written about is disclosed, none of whom were party to the matter **harm ends**

## Additional Context

This is the project's best idea colliding with its new threat model. Separate notebooks would allow producing one notebook; the design that deleted the hardest requirement (moving ranges between notebooks) also deleted the ability to segregate. Structural, not incidental.
