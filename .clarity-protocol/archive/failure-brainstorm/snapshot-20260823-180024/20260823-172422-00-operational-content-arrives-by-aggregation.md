# Operational content arrives by aggregation

**Source:** conversation

The rule 'never write anything operational' is applied per entry, but identification emerges across entries. Automatic dating, the association graph, and imported documents combine so that three individually innocuous passages identify a person that none of them names. The rule is sound and the mechanism that defeats it is invisible at write time.

## Additional Context

This is why the failure is not a lapse of judgement. The writer persona is zero-decision, mid-thought, at 130 WPM; aggregation is not perceptible from inside a single entry, and no amount of discipline at write time can see it.
