# A gag order removes any chance to warn those affected

**Source:** conversation

Disclosure happens under an order barring the provider from telling the account holder. The user continues writing into a corpus already in an adversary's hands, and cannot warn the people written about that their information has been produced.

## Additional Context

This is what makes the harm to third parties unmitigable rather than merely serious: the one protective action available — telling them — is foreclosed. It also means absence of evidence of disclosure is not evidence of absence.
