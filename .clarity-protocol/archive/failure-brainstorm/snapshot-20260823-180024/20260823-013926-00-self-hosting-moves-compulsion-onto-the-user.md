# Self-hosting moves compulsion onto the user

**Source:** conversation

Removing the third party removes the third party's ability to resist. A hosted provider may litigate, narrow, delay or publish transparency reports; a box in the user's house is seized alongside the laptop, and compulsion is applied directly to the user under contempt.

## Additional Context

Recorded as a counterweight because the instinct that self-hosting is strictly safer is not obviously right. It changes who can be compelled and how, and the answer depends on jurisdiction and adversary, not on architecture alone.
