# Auto-expiry destroys something that mattered

**Source:** conversation

Content that deletes itself on a timer will eventually delete a piece of reasoning the writer had not finished with, or had not yet promoted. This is 'losing anything, ever' as a designed behaviour rather than a defect, and the loss is silent and unrecoverable by construction.
