# Sync credentials on a compromised device reach the hub

**Source:** conversation

The phone or laptop holds credentials that grant read and write access to the full remote corpus. Compromise or seizure of the device therefore yields not just the local replica but everything on the hub, including material purged locally but not remotely.

## Additional Context

T4 already flags Android TLS trust as Portal's one unclosed hole. Under this threat model it stops being a correctness concern and becomes an access-control one.
