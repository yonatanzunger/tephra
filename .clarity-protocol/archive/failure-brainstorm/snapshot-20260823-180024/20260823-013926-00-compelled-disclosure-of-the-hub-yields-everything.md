# Compelled disclosure of the hub yields everything

**Source:** conversation

A hosted remote is served with lawful process and produces the full repository: twenty years of prose, every version, and every passage ever deleted. The user is not the only one harmed — everyone written about is disclosed, none of whom knew the notebook existed.

## Failure Chain

1. v2a pushes the corpus to a hosted remote
2. The provider receives compulsory process naming the account
3. The repository is produced in full, including history and deleted material **harm begins**
4. Third parties discussed in passing are exposed with no notice and no recourse **harm ends**
