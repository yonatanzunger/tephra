Where the frame is decided

The question is not whether there is a sidebar. It is whether the text moves when the sidebar does. A column centred in the space left over beside a nav will shift every line the moment that nav appears, and no amount of care elsewhere makes that acceptable to read.

Centring in a fixed frame costs some width and buys the guarantee outright. Overlaying costs nothing and buys the same guarantee by a different route, at the price of the nav covering the text while it is open. Both are defensible; they are not the same choice.

The gutter and the nav want the same pixels.

The annotation gutter is reserved in all of these, which is the constraint that cannot be added later — marginal notes arriving one day must not reflow twenty years of writing.
