Friday 21 August

Where the margin goes when there is none

The Aldine page put commentary beside the text, and the tradition it borrowed from — marginalia, and behind that the Talmudic page — treats the gloss as part of the document rather than as a note about it. A phone has no beside.

The margin was never decoration. Losing it on the small screen means choosing which of its properties to keep.

Greek and Cyrillic and Hebrew all appear here in ordinary use: ἀρχὴ ἥμισυ παντός, всё гениальное просто, and בראשית ברא אלהים — which at this size is where per-script adjustment stops being a refinement.

Hebrew sits entirely at x-height, so it reads smaller than the Latin beside it even when it is nominally the same size.

The question this sheet exists to answer is narrower than it looks: not which of these is prettiest, but which one you can still read a week from now with one hand on a train.
